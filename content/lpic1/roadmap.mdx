# LPIC-1 Roadmap

Two ways to use this page:

- **Teaching track** — 16 core sessions plus two supplemental process labs, each with a goal, reading, lab, and homework.
- **Exam track** — a countdown plan for solo study toward `101-500` and `102-500`.

Both point at the same lesson pages. Status column tells you what already exists here.

| Status | Meaning |
| ------ | ------- |
| ✅ | Full lesson written |

## Exam facts (know these before planning)

| | 101-500 | 102-500 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Questions | 60 | 60 |
| Time | 90 min | 90 min |
| Pass score | 500 / 800 | 500 / 800 |
| Topics | 101–104 | 105–110 |
| Validity | 5 years | 5 years |

You must pass **both** exams to be LPIC-1 certified. They can be taken in any order and months apart.

<Callout type="warn">
Weight numbers below are LPI's official objective weights. They tell you where the questions actually are — `103` alone is 26 points of exam 101. Study time should follow weight, not interest.
</Callout>

### Last 7 days before either exam

1. Re-run every quiz on this site until you score 100%, not 70%.
2. Rewrite the cheat-sheets from memory on blank paper — recall, not re-reading.
3. Do one timed 60-question mock at the same hour of day as your real exam slot.
4. Review only what you got wrong. Do not start new topics.

## Study rules that actually matter

<Callout type="warn">
**Type every command.** LPIC-1 asks you to *fill in* commands, not only pick them from a list. Reading a cheat-sheet does not build that recall.
</Callout>

- **Two distros, always.** Every lab runs on Debian-family *and* RHEL-family. The exam tests `apt` and `dnf`, `/etc/network/interfaces` and NetworkManager.
- **Break things on purpose.** Corrupt an `fstab`, delete a GRUB entry, lock yourself out of SSH — then recover. That's what the troubleshooting questions are.
- **`man` before Google.** The exam room has no browser. Practice `man -k`, `apropos`, `/usr/share/doc`.
- **AI is a tutor, not an answer key.** Use it to explain a flag you already ran — see [Using AI as a Linux Assistant](/lpic1/linux-ai).
